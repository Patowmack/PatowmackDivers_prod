2025 Meetings

Monday, December 1, 2025

Our December meeting will be our annual Holiday Party on the first Monday, December 1 at 7pm sharp; this gala event is open to club members in good standing and their guests.

This meeting is in-person only at Mylo's Restaurant. For this meeting, we will not have a Zoom space operating. We will cover club business at ~7:30 PM (or sometime after folks eat).

After club business, we will have a Yankee Swap style Secret Santa gift exchange. Hilarity will ensue!

Monday, November 3, 2025

Our November meeting will be on the first Monday, November 3, as usual.

Those who wish to join in person can do so at Mylo's Restaurant. As we have done before, we will have a Zoom space operating for joining virtually. We will cover club business (including discussion of the annual election of club officers) at ~7:30 PM (or sometime after folks eat).

After club business, we will have a presentation from Paul McIlvaine about his efforts pertaining to the removal of derelict crab pots in the state of Delaware.

Monday, August 7, 2025

At 8:30 PM, we will have the chance to enjoy a special presentation from David Walter, P.E., PMP, Director of Marine Operations at University of Delaware.

"Come take a virtual dive in the research submersible ALVIN with David Walter, former lead mechanic and pilot. We'll talk about the history of deep submergence, upgrades to Alvin over the decades, and see some of the more interesting sites in the benthic zone of the oceans around the globe."
